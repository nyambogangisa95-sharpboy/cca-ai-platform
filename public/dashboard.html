Overview

This dashboard provides quick access to client records, active projects, upcoming deadlines, revenue summaries, and AI-driven insights. The backend endpoints are placeholders — connect your data sources to visualize real metrics.

Client & Project Management

  • Store and view client profiles and contact details.
  • Track projects, progress, and deadlines.
  • Appointments, invoices, and payment statuses.
  • Quick actions: message client, create invoice, schedule call.

Analytics & Reporting

  • Traffic and conversion summaries.
  • Revenue by client and campaign.
  • Activity feed (client messages, tasks completed).
  • Exportable performance reports (CSV/PDF).

AI Insights
